# Arianne — Opening Cinematic (English)

37 seconds. Same artwork, audio, scene timings and transitions as the Turkish edition. No voice-over.

## The journey / 0.8–7.8 seconds

Arianne set out for the world's highest mountain
to fulfill her mother's final wish.

## A keepsake / 7.8–12.8 seconds

She carried a bracelet
her mother had left behind.

## The fracture / 12.8–16.8 seconds

As she neared the summit...
the mountain trembled.

## The blue rope / 16.8–22.8 seconds

A blue rope unfurled from the bracelet.
But it could not stop her fall.

## The knots / 22.8–28.8 seconds

She awoke in a forgotten cavern.
The rope was knotted around the rocks.

## The way up / 28.8–35.8 seconds

Above her, a wish remained unfulfilled.
Before her, knots waited to be untied.

## Included files

- Arianne-Opening-EN.mp4: video preview.
- Preview.html and opening-data.js: offline interactive preview; keep both files together.
- Storyboard-EN.png: all six scenes with English captions and timings.
- assets/: unchanged text-free artwork, English full-screen cards and the original audio.
- Scenes-EN.json: editable captions and timings.
- Opening-EN.srt: English subtitles.

The text-free artwork was originally created using the built-in ImageGen tool. This edition reuses those exact images.
